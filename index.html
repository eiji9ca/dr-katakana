📖 How to Play
  • Match 3 or more of the same Japanese sound in a line (horizontal or vertical) to clear pieces.
  • Line up falling Katakana pills with matching Hiragana viruses to eliminate them.
  • Clear all viruses in the bottle to complete the level!
  • Avoid letting capsules stack up to the top entrance chute.
